# Business Pricing Model and Executive Summary: Lanka EMR

This document provides a SaaS business pricing plan, deployment service fees, and an executive summary designed for presentation to hospital management boards and investors.

---

## 1. SaaS Pricing Models

We propose two monetization strategies tailored for South Asian private healthcare providers:

### Option A: Pure Monthly Subscription (SaaS Model)
Best suited for clinics and hospitals wanting minimal upfront costs. Pricing scales based on active user volume (number of doctors, nurses, and admins accessing the portal).

| Tier | Hospital Profile | Active User Volume | Estimated Pricing (Monthly) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clinic / OPD** | Small GP clinics, dental surgeries, pediatric practices | Up to 15 users | **$199 / month** |
| **Mid-Market Hospital** | Multi-specialty private clinics, regional nursing homes | 15–75 users | **$799 / month** |
| **Enterprise Hospital** | Large multi-branch private hospital groups | 75+ users | **Custom quote** (Volume discounts) |

*Features included in all tiers:*
- Unlimited patient registrations and charts.
- Sandbox training portal for onboarding.
- Daily database backups.
- SLA-backed helpdesk support.

---

### Option B: Setup Fee + Monthly Maintenance (Enterprise Model)
Best suited for private hospitals with legacy data, requiring custom integrations with existing diagnostic hardware or localized laboratory equipment.

#### 1. Setup Phase Fees (One-time)
*   **System Deployment & Provisioning**: **$5,000**
    *   Setup of AWS/GCP hosting containers, domain mapping, security certificates, and DB provisioning.
*   **Legacy Data Migration**: **$2,500 - $7,500** (depends on data size)
    *   Exporting, sanitizing, and migrating historical records (patients, history, clinic logs) from legacy systems.
*   **Clinician Onboarding & Training**: **$1,500**
    *   Practical workshop sessions for doctors, nurses, receptionists, and pharmacists, including user reference manuals.

#### 2. Monthly Maintenance Phase Fees (Recurring)
*   **Base Operations Support**: **$499 / month**
    *   24/7 technical helpdesk, security patches, package upgrades.
*   **Infrastructure Hosting Reimbursement**: Pass-through billing based on monthly cloud provider invoices.

## 2. Executive Summary

### Why Lanka EMR?
Traditional Electronic Medical Record (EMR) systems are complex, visually outdated, and difficult to manage. Lanka EMR offers a modern solution tailored to South Asian clinics and hospitals.

### Core Value Drivers for Private Hospitals
1.  **Optimized Clinical Workflows**: Integrated dashboard screens accelerate receptionist registrations, vital signs checks, and doctor consultations, allowing clinics to treat more patients per day.
2.  **Reduced Overhead Costs**: Replacing physical charts, printouts, and folders reduces paper costs, clerical labor, and physical archive storage space.
3.  **Strict Security & HIPAA Compliance**: Dynamic permissions aspects verify credentials before allowing access to records. Patient access logs prevent unauthorized lookups, protecting the hospital from liability.
4.  **Flexible Scaling**: Deployed via high-availability cloud microservices, the system easily scales from a single clinic to a multi-branch network.
5.  **AI-Ready Infrastructure**: Designed with structured JSON clinical data schemas, Lanka EMR is ready to support AI-driven diagnostics, automated chart notes drafting, and smart inventory alerts.
